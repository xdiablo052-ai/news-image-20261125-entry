A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125) entry for **news-image-20261125**: one image that sums up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026.

The poster is a newspaper front page in a single frame, the date named in full at the top:

- **The Gulf** — Iran says ships hit underwater mines in the Strait of Hormuz and warns vessels to coordinate passage
- **Disaster** — a fuel tanker crash ignites a massive fire on a highway outside Cairo, killing at least 31 people
- **Space** — Shenzhou-24 docks at China's space station carrying a Pakistani national, the first foreign astronaut aboard Tiangong
- **Trade** — the US proposes surprise new tariffs on Brazilian imports, including a 10% duty on aircraft

Ticker: Houthis claim Saudi refinery hit · Eurostar hit by strikes · Huawei convicted · Lukoil sold to Boehly · Bangladesh raises garment wage.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory, checking it against the gate's 1 MiB limit first. The image is 1024×1024 PNG, drawn as a poster and rendered locally.
